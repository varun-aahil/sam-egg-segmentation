1. Task 1: SAM Ground Truth Generation via Interactive Point Prompts

1.1 Methodology & Ground Truth Reliability

Meta's Segment Anything Model (SAM) with the vit_b backbone was deployed on an NVIDIA GPU (RTX 4070 Laptop GPU, CUDA 12.8 acceleration). Rather than relying on unguided automatic mask generation with hand-tuned heuristic post-filters (which missed eggs in boiling water pots and produced 13-19 false-positive leaf highlights in foliage scenes), we built an interactive OpenCV annotation tool (src/annotate_points.py).

Using src/annotate_points.py, human point prompts (positive eggshell foreground clicks and background negative clicks) were recorded for every image and persisted to points.json. In src/sam_segment.py, SamPredictor consumed these point coordinates with multimask_output=True, selecting the highest-confidence mask per egg. Each instance is encoded with an integer label ID (0 = background, 1..n = eggs). All hand-tuned shape and color filters were removed from the ground-truth path, ensuring high-fidelity, unbiased ground truth masks across all 110 images.

1.3 Remaining SAM Challenges & Artifacts

  • Extreme Backlighting: When an egg is backlit by an intense incandescent lamp, the severe lens glare and saturated background create faint halo fringes around the silhouette.
  • Refraction & Boiling Turbulence: Images with eggs submerged in vigorously boiling water exhibit rippling refraction boundaries, causing minor boundary uncertainty at the water-air-shell interface.

2. Task 2: Classical Image-Processing Segmentation

2.1 Evolutionary Engineering Process

The classical pipeline was implemented as a single modular parameterized function (segment_pipeline) in src/classical_segment.py. We executed 15 structured, logged trials exclusively on the 88 dev images, logging decisions (KEEP vs DISCARD) and reasons dynamically at runtime based on measured dev IoU relative to the running best:

  1. baseline: Grayscale conversion + Gaussian blur (5x5) + Otsu threshold + hole fill + min area 500 px. Establishes baseline dev IoU of 0.3521 and Dice 0.4700.
  2. otsu_inv: Inverted Otsu thresholding. Severely degrades (IoU 0.1040), proving eggshell luminance is generally higher than background.
  3. clahe_otsu: CLAHE contrast equalization (clip 2.0). Dropped IoU to 0.2601 by over-amplifying background wood grain and marble veining.
  4. adaptive_thresh: Adaptive Gaussian thresholding. Catastrophic failure (IoU 0.0638); the local block window hollowed out large egg centers into thin annular rings.
  5. hsv_sat: HSV color gating (S < 70, V > 130). Improved IoU to 0.3593 (+0.0073, KEEP) by targeting pale eggshell chroma.
  6. lab_l: CIELAB perceptual lightness L* channel Otsu. Dropped to 0.3266 due to lower contrast against warm table surfaces.
  7. morph_small (7x7 ellipse): Elliptical opening and closing cleaned fine speckles (IoU 0.3566).
  8. morph_medium (15x15 ellipse): Scaled structuring element closer to egg curvature, advancing IoU to 0.3769 (+0.0175, KEEP).
  9. morph_large (25x25 ellipse): Kernel matched natural egg convexity, smoothing contours and reaching IoU 0.3973 (+0.0204, KEEP).
  10. border_polarity: Border-adaptive threshold inversion. IoU 0.3784; helpful for backlit silhouettes but slightly penalized bright table surfaces.
  11. lab_border_dist: Euclidean distance to estimated border background color. Reached IoU 0.3584.
  12. component_ranking: Scoring connected components by area, solidity, ellipse aspect ratio, and center distance. Reached IoU 0.3973.
  13. watershed_conditional: Applying distance-transform watershed conditionally when components fail convexity criteria. Reached IoU 0.3973.
  14. watershed_always: Distance transform + watershed across all detected egg clusters. Reached IoU 0.3975 (+0.0003, KEEP), successfully splitting touching egg instances.
  15. final: Grayscale Otsu + large morph (25x25) + GrabCut iterative refinement + distance watershed. Reached highest dev IoU 0.4008 and Dice 0.5151 (KEEP).

3.4 Failure Analysis Grouped by Physical Root Cause

  • Boiling Pot Water & Specular Rim Reflections (e.g. not_damaged_125, 42, 123): In cooking pots, turbulent boiling water and bright metallic rim reflections exhibit high luminance across 85%+ of the image frame. Otsu treats the surrounding liquid/rim as foreground, obscuring the submerged egg. Improvement: Circle Hough transform to localize pot boundaries, followed by high-gradient specular rejection.
  • Hand Skin & Cast Shadow Color Camouflage (e.g. not_damaged_24, 20): When an egg is held in fingers or resting on warm polished wood, hand skin and warm timber share nearly identical luminance and Hue/Saturation values with light-brown eggshells. Global intensity thresholding leaks onto adjacent fingers. Improvement: Learned skin-color probability maps (Cr/Cb color gating) to mask out human skin prior to thresholding.
  • Severe Silhouette Backlighting / Reverse Polarity (e.g. not_damaged_44): When an egg is held directly in front of a bright window or lamp, the egg appears completely in shadow (dark core) surrounded by an over-exposed bright background. Standard Otsu selects the background glare as foreground. Improvement: Border-adaptive polarity switching coupled with gradient-energy contour snake active contours.
  • High-Frequency Foliage & Herb Leaf Highlights (e.g. not_damaged_145, 117): Eggs placed among green herb leaves (basil/coriander) create dozens of tiny specular leaf highlights that pass initial intensity gating, fragmenting the watershed. Improvement: Excess-Green index subtraction (2G - R - B) to suppress botanical foliage before thresholding.

4. Task 4: Video Tracking

4.1 Problem Diagnosis & Resolution

The input demonstration video (video/eggs.mp4, 1280x720 @ 24 fps, 1440 frames) demonstrates the difference between a raw egg and a hard-boiled egg on a wooden tabletop. Initial review of the baseline tracker revealed severe ID fragmentation, where sample frame ~600 showed IDs Egg 5 and Egg 6 for a scene with only two eggs. Our diagnostic inspection uncovered three root causes:

  1. Missing Touching-Instance Separation: At frames 235-250, the demonstrator places the two eggs immediately adjacent to each other. Without watershed instance separation, the baseline algorithm merged them into a single blob (~37,500 px), dropping one track.
  2. Insufficient Track Memory (max_lost = 15): Hand occlusions during the spin demonstration lasted up to 43 consecutive frames (1.8 seconds). Setting max_lost = 15 caused tracks to be purged prematurely, minting new IDs upon re-emergence.
  3. Absence of Motion Prediction: During occlusion, static track centroids failed to anticipate displacement during high-velocity spins across the table.

We solved this by integrating Euclidean distance-transform watershed (ws_min_dist = 60) directly into video segmentation, extending occlusion memory to max_lost = 90 frames, and implementing constant-velocity prediction with decay damping during occlusions.
