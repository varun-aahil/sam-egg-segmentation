1. Executive Summary

This report details an end-to-end computer vision solution for egg segmentation and multi-object tracking. Ground truth masks were established via SAM (vit_b) with rigorous quality auditing. A classical segmentation pipeline was built via controlled single-parameter hypotheses evaluated solely on the dev split. The frozen final configuration was tested once on the eval split. Finally, a robust multi-object tracker was deployed on a 1440-frame video test sequence, achieving 0 ID switches and resolving challenging hand occlusions.

2. Task 1: Ground Truth Mask Generation (SAM)

SAM (vit_b) was executed on CUDA (NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Laptop GPU) using saved point prompts from points.json. An automated audit in src/contact_sheet.py flagged masks with area > 40% or edge contact > 50%. Three boiling-pot images with flooded masks (not_damaged_124, 135, 145) were corrected with tight bounding boxes, reducing mask areas from ~80% down to ~13-14% (0 flagged masks remaining).

Ground Truth Egg Distribution across 110 images: 93 images (84.5%) with 1 egg; 6 images (5.5%) with 2 eggs; 11 images (10.0%) with 3 eggs.

5. Task 4: Multi-Object Video Tracking

Tracking was evaluated on the 1440-frame table sequence (1280x720 @ 24.0 fps). Eggs are placed on the table at frame 222 and spun. The tracker maintains persistent IDs using Hungarian matching on IoU + distance with constant-velocity prediction.

  • Fingertip Leakage Resolution: At frame 600, demonstrator hand contact caused baseline thresholding to leak into fingers. Incorporating YCrCb skin-color gating (Cr ∈ [135, 175], Cb ∈ [80, 125]) and larger opening eliminated 2,296 px of false skin contour.
  • Dynamic Switch Measurement: 0 ID switches were measured across all 1440 frames.
  • Intro/Outro Robustness: 658 frames with 0 detections were handled smoothly without track hallucination.

6. Interview Preparation & Technical Discussion

Why classical segmentation struggled on boiling pots and white trays

Global and adaptive thresholding methods fundamentally rely on contrast gradients. On white styrofoam trays and white bowls, the contrast gradient between the eggshell and surface is near-zero (ΔL* ≈ 0–5). In boiling pots, rapid bubble refraction and boiling water motion break the assumed smooth convex geometry of the egg boundary.

Why Hungarian matching was chosen over DeepSORT

Eggs are smooth, rotationally symmetric, textureless objects. Feature embeddings from deep CNN re-identification models (like DeepSORT) provide virtually zero distinctiveness between individual white eggs. Hungarian matching on spatial centroid distance combined with constant-velocity motion forecasting provides a mathematically sound, lightweight, and robust tracking solution without neural re-ID overhead.

What would you do next with more time?

  1. Deep Semantic Segmentation: Fine-tune a YOLOv8-seg or Mask2Former architecture on the dev split using SAM pseudo-masks. Deep CNNs encode contextual priors that effortlessly separate eggs from white trays.
  2. Active Contours / Snakes: Apply Chan-Vese energy minimization with an elliptical shape prior to snap onto faint contrast boundaries on low-contrast trays.
  3. Optical Flow Association: Integrate dense Farneback optical flow into the video tracker to track eggs through rapid hand occlusions during high-speed spinning.
